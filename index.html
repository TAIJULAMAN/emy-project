Quick guidance

Open each question to learn more about how EMY works for customers and businesses.

What is EMY?

EMY is a local discovery platform that helps people find nearby businesses, explore services, and connect directly with providers in their area.

How does EMY help users?

Users can search for businesses, view services, compare local options, ask questions, and interact with businesses more easily from one place.

Can businesses create their own profile?

Yes. Businesses can create or manage a profile, add key information, publish services, upload images, and make it easier for local customers to find them.

Is EMY free for businesses?

EMY may offer free and paid business features depending on profile visibility, subscriptions, promotions, and additional tools available on the platform.

Does EMY use my location?

EMY may use approximate or precise location data where permission is granted, mainly to show nearby businesses and improve local recommendations.

How do I contact support?

You can contact EMY through the contact form for help with accounts, business profiles, privacy requests, technical issues, or general platform support.

Privacy centre

Your data, clearly explained.

EMY uses information to power local discovery, business connections, support, safety, and personalised platform experiences.

What we collect

Account details, contact information, business profiles, messages, location signals, device data, usage activity, and content you choose to share.

Why we use it

To run EMY, help people discover nearby businesses, improve recommendations, protect the platform, support users, and provide business tools.

Who may receive it

Businesses you interact with, trusted service providers, payment partners, analytics tools, security providers, and authorities where required by law.

Data categories

EMY may process account data, business data, location data, usage data, user content, and transaction data depending on which features you use.

Your choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, cookie choices, location permissions, and marketing preference changes.

Security, AI, and retention

EMY may use automated tools and AI-supported systems to suggest businesses, services, content, or support responses. Data is kept only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent misuse, and maintain business records.
